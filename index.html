Me, Myself,
and I

2025  ·  Digital Poster Series  ·  Photoshop · Adobe Firefly

A four-poster series exploring design principles through Ethereal Romantic Maximalism — transforming skeletal imagery into contemplative studies of emotion and beauty. United by burgundy, gold, and bone white, each poster challenges conventional memento mori treatment by rendering death imagery with tenderness rather than aggression.

Four Posters

Form and Fracture

An exploration of structural disintegration. The form breaks apart with deliberate precision, asking where an object ends and its absence begins — design as the space between presence and disappearance

Identity

A celebration of desi heritage through henna-adorned hands. Gold accents evoke kintsugi philosophy — beauty through repair — while the intricate patterns assert cultural identity as something both decorative and deeply structural.

Impact

A dramatic falling skeleton with vertical typography. The composition captures the moment of collision — a study in kinetic energy, contrast, and the visual weight of consequence.

Feeling

A dissolving painterly skull embodying quiet restraint. The figure fades at its edges as though made of smoke — evoking emotion as something that exists just beyond what can be held.
